# Principles

How to choose when two bets compete. The strategist cites these by heading.

## Unblock others first

A review or answer someone is waiting on outranks own work of equal weight.

## Only rocks get bet

Sand gets shaped and water gets a spike before either competes for time.

## No backlog

Unbet work does not queue. It becomes a bet or it is dropped.

## End-to-end or not done

A bet counts as landed when it works through the real flow, not when its
tasks show done.
